"Corporate employees traveling on business trips or client-site project deployments often lose productive hours switching across multiple apps to track meeting schedules, extract venue details from emails, compare local commute options, and navigate an unfamiliar city. This project solves that friction by providing an AI-powered Personal Travel & Work Assistant that connects to Google Calendar and Gmail to automate meeting-to-meeting commute fare comparisons (across Rapido, Ola, Uber, and GREEN SM), surface nearby attractions for downtime, and offer quick personal wellness/cultural touchpoints—all from a single conversational interface."
